What this can and cannot do. It ranks strategies well and picks the right number of stops at most circuits. It cannot tell you a gap in seconds reliably, and it cannot tell soft tyres from hard ones, because the noise between two identical stints is ten times bigger than the difference.

  • Alternatives assume everyone else does exactly what they really did.
  • The model knows a safety car happened. The driver did not.
  • Canada is missing: its measured tyre wear is negative, which is impossible.
  • No wet races, and no tyre temperatures or pressures, which are not public.
